# User Research & Product Discovery

This document summarizes the user research and discovery work that informed the development of the Herominutes MVP.

The goal of this research was to better understand how athletes approach injury recovery, where existing tools fall short, and what product capabilities would improve recovery adherence and outcomes.

---

# Research Objectives

The research focused on answering several key questions:

• How do athletes currently manage injury recovery?  
• What challenges do they experience during rehabilitation?  
• What causes recovery programs to fail or be abandoned?  
• What tools or support systems do athletes wish existed?

The insights gathered helped shape the design of the Herominutes recovery platform.

---

# User Feedback Themes

Several recurring themes emerged during early research and conversations with athletes.

## 1. Recovery Programs Lack Structure

Many athletes receive recovery exercises from physical therapists but struggle to maintain a clear structure for completing them consistently.

Common feedback included:

• exercises written on paper or scattered across notes  
• difficulty remembering routines  
• lack of session structure  

This insight led to the design of **guided recovery workflows** within Herominutes.

---

## 2. Motivation Drops Over Time

Athletes often start rehabilitation programs with high motivation but lose consistency after the initial phase.

Key reasons included:

• recovery progress feels slow  
• lack of feedback or progress tracking  
• routines feel repetitive or boring  

This insight influenced the introduction of:

• progress tracking  
• streak systems  
• gamification elements  
• milestone celebrations

---

## 3. Pain Changes Daily

Pain levels and mobility limitations fluctuate throughout recovery.

Athletes expressed frustration with static programs that do not adapt to how they feel on a given day.

This led to the introduction of a **pain feedback system** allowing the platform to adjust recommendations based on user input.

---

## 4. Mobility Work Is Often Neglected

Many athletes skip stretching and mobility routines because they feel optional or unstructured.

However, athletes consistently acknowledged that mobility work improved recovery and performance when done regularly.

This insight led to the inclusion of a **stretching and flow movement system** within the Herominutes MVP.

# User Personas

## Recovering Athlete

Profile:

• previously active in sports or training  
• currently recovering from injury  
• motivated to return to performance  

Needs:

• structured recovery program  
• progress tracking  
• safe exercise guidance  

Challenges:

• staying consistent with recovery exercises  
• knowing when to progress  
• managing pain fluctuations

---

## Mobility-Focused Athlete

Profile:

• active individual focused on flexibility and injury prevention  
• interested in mobility and stretching routines  

Needs:

• guided mobility flows  
• warm-up and cool-down routines  
• performance support  

Challenges:

• remembering to do mobility consistently  
• finding routines tailored to their body or sport

---

# Customer Journey

## Stage 1 — Injury Occurs

The athlete experiences an injury and begins searching for ways to recover safely.

Pain, uncertainty, and frustration are common emotions during this stage.

---

## Stage 2 — Recovery Plan Begins

The athlete receives exercises or recovery recommendations but may struggle with consistency and structure.

Pain levels and motivation fluctuate.

---

## Stage 3 — Recovery Progress

As recovery improves, the athlete begins rebuilding strength and mobility.

At this stage, structured routines and progress tracking become especially valuable.

---

## Stage 4 — Return to Performance

The athlete regains confidence and begins returning to training or sport.

Ongoing mobility and recovery support can help prevent reinjury.

---

# Product Insight

The research revealed that recovery success depends on three factors:

• structured recovery routines  
• adaptive guidance based on user feedback  
• motivation systems that encourage consistency

Herominutes was designed to combine these elements into a single platform that supports athletes throughout the recovery journey.

---

# Impact on MVP Design

Insights from user research directly influenced several core product decisions:

• guided recovery workflows  
• stretching and mobility routines  
• pain feedback system  
• gamification and streak tracking  
• personalized recovery program generation

These elements form the foundation of the Herominutes MVP.
