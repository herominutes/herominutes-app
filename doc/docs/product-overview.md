# Herominutes Product Overview

Herominutes is an AI-powered injury recovery and mobility platform designed to help athletes recover safely, stay consistent with rehabilitation, and return to performance with confidence.

The platform combines structured recovery programs, mobility flows, adaptive AI recommendations, and gamified progress systems to support athletes throughout the recovery journey.

---

# Problem

Athletes recovering from injury face several common challenges:

• recovery programs are often unstructured  
• exercises are difficult to remember or follow consistently  
• pain levels change daily but programs do not adapt  
• motivation declines during long recovery periods  

Many athletes abandon recovery programs before completing them, increasing the risk of reinjury.

---

# Solution

Herominutes provides a guided recovery platform that helps athletes stay consistent and progress safely through rehabilitation.

Key capabilities include:

• personalized injury recovery programs  
• guided stretching and mobility flows  
• adaptive pain feedback system  
• AI-powered recovery insights  
• gamified progression and streak tracking  

The goal is to make recovery feel structured, motivating, and personalized.

---

# Core Product Areas

The MVP focuses on two primary areas of athlete recovery.

## Injury Recovery

Structured rehabilitation sessions designed to help athletes recover from injuries and rebuild strength safely.

Features include:

• recovery plan generation  
• guided exercise sessions  
• pain-informed modifications  
• progress tracking

---

## Stretching & Flow Movement

Mobility routines designed to support recovery, flexibility, and injury prevention.

Features include:

• guided mobility flows  
• warm-up and cool-down routines  
• flexibility progression  
• recovery-supportive stretching

---

# AI System

Herominutes uses a multi-specialist AI system to support personalized recovery guidance.

The platform includes:

• 7 injury recovery specialists  
• 7 stretching and mobility specialists  

These specialists help generate customized recovery recommendations based on athlete inputs and recovery progress.

The system also explores **tiered AI generation**:

Fast AI — quick guidance and recommendations  
Medium AI — contextual adjustments and recovery insights  
Deep AI — personalized recovery plan generation

---

# Gamification & Progression

To improve consistency and engagement, the platform incorporates behavioral motivation systems including:

• streak tracking  
• recovery milestones  
• leveling systems  
• achievement badges  

These systems encourage athletes to stay consistent throughout the recovery process.

---

# Target Users

The platform is designed primarily for:

• athletes recovering from injury  
• active individuals seeking mobility improvement  
• people focused on injury prevention and performance longevity

---

# Product Vision

The long-term vision for Herominutes is to become an intelligent recovery platform that helps athletes:

• recover faster and more safely  
• build consistent recovery habits  
• improve mobility and movement quality  
• prevent future injuries

The platform aims to combine product design, recovery science, and adaptive AI to support the full athlete recovery journey.
