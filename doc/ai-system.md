# Herominutes AI System

Herominutes uses a multi-specialist AI system designed to support athletes through personalized injury recovery and mobility progression.

The platform combines structured recovery workflows, specialist-driven recommendations, and adaptive AI generation depth to create a more personalized and engaging recovery experience.

---

## AI System Overview

The Herominutes AI system is built around two specialist groups:

### Injury Recovery Specialists
A set of 7 AI specialists focused on helping athletes recover from injury safely and progressively.

### Stretching & Flow Movement Specialists
A set of 7 AI specialists focused on mobility, flexibility, movement quality, and recovery-supportive stretching routines.

Together, these specialists support a more adaptive and athlete-centered recovery experience.

---

## 1. Injury Recovery Specialist System

The injury recovery system includes 7 specialist roles that contribute to customized recovery guidance.

Examples of specialist focus areas include:

• injury assessment and recovery path guidance  
• rehabilitation protocol support  
• pain-informed exercise adaptation  
• return-to-performance planning  
• recovery progression monitoring  
• athlete adherence and consistency support  
• recovery analytics and insight generation  

These specialists help create more structured and personalized injury recovery programs for athletes.

---

## 2. Stretching & Flow Movement Specialist System

The stretching and flow movement system includes 7 specialist roles focused on mobility and movement support.

Examples of specialist focus areas include:

• mobility flow design  
• flexibility progression support  
• movement quality improvement  
• warm-up and cool-down support  
• body-region-specific stretching recommendations  
• recovery-supportive flow routines  
• athlete mobility consistency and progression tracking  

These specialists are designed to improve flexibility, movement readiness, and long-term recovery support.

---

## 3. Tiered AI Generation Model

Herominutes explores a tiered AI generation system based on different levels of depth and complexity.

### Fast Generation
Designed for quick, lightweight recommendations and lower-friction user support.

Example use cases:

• quick daily check-ins  
• simple recovery suggestions  
• basic mobility recommendations  
• fast session guidance  

### Medium Generation
Designed for more personalized and context-aware recovery support.

Example use cases:

• exercise adjustments  
• mobility flow refinement  
• progress-based recovery suggestions  
• pain-informed modifications  

### Deep Generation
Designed for richer reasoning and more advanced personalization.

Example use cases:

• customized recovery program generation  
• complex athlete recovery planning  
• multi-factor pain and progress interpretation  
• more advanced adaptive recovery guidance  

This tiered model allows Herominutes to balance speed, personalization, and depth of reasoning.

---

## 4. Customized Recovery Programs for Athletes

A core goal of Herominutes is to generate recovery programs that are customized to the individual athlete.

Personalization inputs may include:

• injury type  
• injury region  
• pain level  
• mobility limitations  
• recovery stage  
• athlete goals  
• sport or training context  
• consistency and adherence history  

The platform is designed to generate more adaptive recovery plans rather than static, one-size-fits-all programs.

---

## 5. Pain Tolerance and Pain Feedback System

Herominutes includes a pain-informed system that helps adapt recommendations based on user feedback.

This system is designed to support:

• daily pain check-ins  
• exercise tolerance feedback  
• safe progression guidance  
• flare-up awareness  
• pain-based exercise modifications  

The purpose of the pain system is to improve personalization while reducing the risk of overloading the athlete during recovery.

---

## 6. Gamification and Leveling System

Herominutes explores gamification as a way to improve motivation, adherence, and consistency.

Core concepts include:

• leveling up through recovery progress  
• streaks and consistency rewards  
• milestone achievements  
• progress celebrations  
• progression-based unlocking of deeper AI support  

The platform uses gamification not only for engagement, but also to reinforce positive recovery habits over time.

---

## 7. AI + Gamification Relationship

One of the unique design ideas behind Herominutes is connecting AI depth and personalization to user progression.

As users engage more consistently, level up, and provide more recovery data, the platform can unlock richer forms of support such as:

• more personalized recovery suggestions  
• more adaptive movement flows  
• deeper AI-generated recovery planning  
• improved progress interpretation  

This creates a system where recovery engagement improves the quality of future AI support.

---

## 8. Product Goal

The long-term goal of the Herominutes AI system is to create an intelligent recovery platform that helps athletes:

• recover more safely  
• stay more consistent  
• improve mobility and movement quality  
• return to performance with greater confidence  

Herominutes is designed to combine structured product design, athlete recovery workflows, specialist AI support, and gamified progression into a single recovery experience.
